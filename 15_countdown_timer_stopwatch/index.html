💡 Machine Coding Timing Mechanics:

  • Why setInterval Drifts: Macrotask queues yield to main-thread tasks, losing 2–15ms per second.
  • Delta Time Correction: Calculating elapsed = Date.now() - startTime guarantees precision.
  • SVG Circumference Calculation: dashoffset = circumference * (1 - progress) where $C = 2 \pi r$.
  • Synthesizing Audio without MP3 files: Generating a finish chime via the AudioContext oscillator API.
